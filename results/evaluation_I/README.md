# Evaluation I: Literature evidence generation
 
Outputs of the literature search and screening steps (Steps 1–5) for six research questions. Each file lists the papers that the system **included at full-text screening** for one question.
 
## Research questions
 
Each filename follows `fulltext_screen_<exposure>_<outcome>_included.csv`.
 
Q1: Determine the association of deprescribing interventions with reducing medication count and PIMs in community-dwelling older adults.

Q2: Evaluate the effects of RAS inhibitors on cardiotoxicity, dyspnea, and quality of life (QOL) in patients with malignancies.

Q3: Evaluate the efficacy of chronotherapy for patients with essential hypertension.

Q4: Which drugs are associated with the greatest efficacy in the treatment of antipsychotic-induced akathisia?

Q5: Assess and compare the efficacy and safety of pharmacological treatments for psychotic depression.

Q6: Examine the efficacy of low-dose quetiapine (300 mg/day or less) in sleep.
